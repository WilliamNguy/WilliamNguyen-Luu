About Me

Hey! I’m William, a Canadian artist based in Montreal, currently completing a BFA Specialization in Computation Arts. My work explores the intersection of art and technology, with a focus on 3D modelling, animation, and digital environments. Using Blender, I’m interested in creating visually engaging work through modelling, lighting, materials, motion, and experimentation.

My practice also extends into creative coding and interactive media, allowing me to explore different ways of combining visual design and technology. This website was designed and developed by me using JavaScript as a space to showcase my work and evolving creative practice.
